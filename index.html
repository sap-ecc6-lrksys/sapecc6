FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about SAP IDES installation.

What is SAP IDES and why do I need it?
+
SAP IDES (Internet Demonstration and Evaluation System) is a pre-configured SAP system loaded with sample company data across all SAP modules. It is essential for SAP learners, trainees, freelancers, and consultants who need a personal practice environment without needing corporate system access.
What are the minimum system requirements for SAP ECC 6.0 EHP8 + Fiori?
+
Minimum: Intel i3/i5/i7 or Ryzen (latest gen), 8 GB RAM (16GB recommended), 400 GB free HDD/SSD space in a single partition, VT-x and Hyper-V virtualization enabled in BIOS, Windows 10/11 64-bit OS. Installation takes 15+ hours without interruption.
Do you provide ghost image or dump copy installations?
+
Absolutely NOT. We only provide genuine and procedural SAP IDES installations. Ghost images and dump copies are unreliable, often corrupt, and difficult to maintain. Our installations are done fresh, step-by-step, ensuring a stable, complete system.
How long does the installation take?
+
A complete genuine procedural SAP IDES ECC 6.0 EHP8 + Fiori installation typically takes 15 hours or more depending on your hardware speed. This is why we recommend having a stable system with good CPU and sufficient RAM. Do not interrupt the process once started.
Do you install SAP IDES outside India — worldwide?
+
Yes! We provide SAP IDES online installation service worldwide via secure remote desktop. We have successfully served clients in USA, UK, UAE, Canada, Australia, Singapore, Germany, Malaysia, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, New Zealand, France, Switzerland, Netherlands, and 30+ more countries. All you need is a qualifying system and a stable internet connection.
Which SAP ECC versions do you install?
+
We install all SAP ECC 6.0 enhancement packages from EHP1 through EHP8, including the latest EHP8 with full Fiori 2.0 Launchpad. We also offer custom version combinations on request.
What support do you provide after installation?
+
We provide follow-up support after installation to ensure your SAP IDES system is working properly. This includes help with initial logon, module verification, and resolving any post-install issues.
How do I book and what is the payment process?
